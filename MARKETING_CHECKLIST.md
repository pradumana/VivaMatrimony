## 📱 Product Context

**Product Name:** Viva  
**Tagline:** "Find someone who feels like home."  
**Category:** Indian Matrimonial Mobile App  
**Platform:** Android/iOS (Flutter)  

**Key Differentiators:**
- Premium experience at low cost (₹800-3,200/month operation)
- WhatsApp-based authentication (familiar, trustworthy)
- Beautiful biodata generation with 6+ traditional templates
- Smart AI-powered matching algorithm
- Reference-based verification system
- Privacy-first design with profile visibility controls
- Clean, modern Material 3 UI

**Target Audience:**
- Age: 21-35 years
- Location: India (all states, focus on urban + Tier-2 cities)
- Demographics: Middle-class families seeking marriage alliances
- Language: English + Hindi (multilingual ready)
- Tech-savvy but value traditional matchmaking values

**Core Features:**
1. WhatsApp OTP login (no complex passwords)
2. Comprehensive profile creation (education, career, family, lifestyle, preferences)
3. Smart matching based on compatibility
4. Send/receive interests with family involvement
5. In-app secure messaging
6. Generate & share beautiful biodata PDFs (6 templates: Modern, Traditional, Royal, Floral, etc.)
7. Reference verification system (family/friends vouch for profiles)
8. Advanced search filters (religion, caste, height, education, location, etc.)
9. Privacy controls (who can see your profile, photos)
10. Admin moderation for safety
